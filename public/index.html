Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Clear answers regarding SCRAP-LINK's inspection station, accuracy boundaries, and warehouse coordination.

Does SCRAP-LINK detect hidden or chemical contamination? ▼
No. The system only screens for visible surface indicators (food residue, foreign non-PET material such as foil or PVC, and visible wetness/liquids). An outcome of "No visible indicator" denotes that no visible cues were observed in the camera frames, but does not certify that the bottle is chemically clean or certified food-grade recyclable.
How does manual rotation work in the prototype? ▼
The initial prototype utilizes a manual turntable platform (Ø78mm) inside the chamber. The software guides the operator to rotate the bottle through 0°, 90°, 180°, and 270° angles to ensure 360° visual coverage. Motorized turntable rotation is designed as a future upgrade.
Why is blockchain excluded from the initial prototype? ▼
Blockchain is treated as an architectural feasibility study. Blockchain cannot detect contamination or validate image focus, nor can it prevent incorrect manual weight entries (the Oracle problem). For a single-laptop local pilot, standard SHA-256 cryptographic hashes provide complete auditability without the high costs, key management, and latency of a distributed blockchain.
How do warehouses receive the sorted materials? ▼
Once a batch is assembled and verified at the collection point, it is offered to participating recycling dealers through the platform. Dealers can review the image proof manifest and bottle count before accepting. Physical handover is tracked separately upon carrier arrival.
